# KINGAI Global Intelligence — Business & Strategic Opportunity

**Product:** https://intel.kingai.work/  
**Business & strategic collaboration:** vip@kingai.work

KINGAI Global Intelligence is positioned as an evidence-linked global intelligence product for people and organizations that need faster comparative context without losing methodological transparency.

This document is public-safe. It describes customer value, commercial directions and strategic opportunity without disclosing proprietary scoring implementation, private collection systems, credentials, internal automation, private topology or unpublished evidence.

## The customer problem

Decision-makers face three recurring problems:

1. **Too much information** — global events, media, institutional data and public discussion are difficult to synthesize consistently.
2. **Too little comparability** — rankings often use different assumptions, dates or criteria, making side-by-side comparison misleading.
3. **Too little transparency** — users are frequently shown a score or conclusion without enough visibility into uncertainty, source provenance or missing data.

KINGAI Global Intelligence aims to reduce that friction by giving users a consistent public framework, visible uncertainty, attributable sources and fast access to country-level context.

## Product value

The public product can create value through:
