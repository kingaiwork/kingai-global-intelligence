# KINGAI Global Intelligence · Public disclosure policy

**Corporate operator:** KINGAIWORK LLC (Wyoming, USA). Official business inquiries: vip@kingai.work; website: https://kingai.work.

The project's public GitHub surface may disclose product capabilities, source provenance, methodologies, correctly attributed data, reproducible public documentation and verified release status. It must not publish private individuals' legal names or personal identity associations, personal email/phone/residential addresses, internal collection credentials, unreleased evidence, security topology or customer records. Public price tables and contract amounts do not belong in company product documentation.

Before a PR is merged, review code, data files, documentation, images, release metadata and any generated site output for personal metadata and other unwanted disclosure. Do not rewrite historical commit metadata without a separate impact review. Respect original author attribution and third-party licensing. For legal company identification see https://github.com/kingaiwork/kingaiwork.
